Task 5: Mock Coding Interview

Set a timer for 60 minutes.

Rules:

Pick any 5 problems from today's list.
Solve them without using AI or looking at solutions.
After finishing, review your code and improve readability.

For each problem, ask yourself:

Did I understand the problem correctly?
Could I solve it without hints?
Is my solution efficient?
Can I explain it to someone else?
